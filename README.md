# ChoiceCorridor
Simple train dilemma game
Choice Corridor

A short first-person narrative experience.

A routine Take Your Child to Work Day at a fertility clinic
turns into a disaster when a fire breaks out.

Trapped in the final corridor, the player must choose
between rescuing a child or preserving a cryogenic storage
unit containing one hundred fertilized embryos.

Every choice carries consequences.

Features:
- Half-Life inspired environmental storytelling
- Multiple endings
- Hidden routes
- Ethical decision-making
- No combat-focused gameplay
